Delete your Slime Trail.io account

Slime Trail.io by VZ Games

You can ask us to delete your Slime Trail.io account and all game data linked to it at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Slime Trail.io and note your Player ID (shown on the Home screen).
  2. Send an email to creatordbz3@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Slime Trail.io account.
  3. Include your Player ID in the email. If you signed in with Google Play Games, also include your Play Games profile name.
  4. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your Player ID, the link to your Google Play Games account, your coins, ranking points, leaderboard position, owned trails, skills and skins, and your match statistics.

What is kept, and for how long

Our server keeps daily backups for up to 14 days. A copy of your data may remain in these backups until they are automatically deleted, at most 14 days after your account is removed. We do not keep anything else.

After deletion your progress cannot be restored. If you play again, a new empty account will be created.

See also our Privacy Policy.
