Privacy Policy

Slime Trail.io by VZ Games · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Slime Trail.io ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by VZ Games, an individual developer.

1. Information the game itself collects

DataWhy
A random Player ID created by the game on your phone To keep your account: coins, ranking points, items you own, your loadout and your player name.
Game activity (matches played, wins, kills, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, name changes) To run the game, give rewards and show the global ranking.
Your Google Play Games player ID (only if you sign in with Google Play Games) To link your progress to your Google Play Games account, so it comes back after reinstalling or on a new phone.

Your player name and ranking points are visible to other players in the ranking and in matches. The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

To play online your phone connects to our game servers, which necessarily see your IP address while you are connected. Our servers do not store your IP address in your account.

Game data sent between your phone and our game servers is not encrypted. It contains only game information (your Player ID, movements, scores), never passwords or payment details.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: banner ads on menu screens, a full-screen ad now and then between matches, and optional "watch an ad" rewards (revive, double coins). Google AdMob may automatically collect and share with Google the following information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (approximate) location
  • Interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views)
  • Diagnostic information (for example app performance)

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used, and you can choose to consent, manage your options or not consent.

3. Google Play Games

Signing in with Google Play Games is handled by Google. When you sign in, Google tells our server your Google Play Games player ID so we can link it to your game account. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How long data is kept

Your account data is kept as long as your account exists. Our servers keep daily backups for up to 14 days. When you delete your account, your data is removed and disappears from the backups within 14 days.

5. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and all game data linked to it at any time. The steps are on our Delete your account page (also reachable from the game: Settings → Delete Account).

6. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Selling data

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with Google as described above (advertising and Google Play Games sign-in).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
