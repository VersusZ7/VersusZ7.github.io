Privacy Policy for Slime Trail.io

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Slime Trail.io ("the game") is made by VZ Games. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information we collect

When you play online, the game automatically creates:

  • A random player ID (a code made by the game, not linked to your real name, email or phone number)
  • An automatic player name (for example "Player8261")
  • Game progress: coins, points, wins, kills, win streak, items you unlock and your chosen trail, skill and skin
  • Results of offline matches, which are added to your account the next time you go online

If you sign in with Google Play Games, we also receive your Google Play Games player ID. We use it only to link your game progress to your Google Play Games account, so you can get your progress back after reinstalling the game or on a new device. We do not receive your email address or password.

We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information. The game has no chat.

2. How we use this information

This information is used only to run the game: to save your coins and items, restore your progress, show the global ranking and leaderboards, and let you play online matches.

3. Where the information is stored

Game progress is stored on our game servers (hosted by DigitalOcean in Germany and India). The player ID is also saved on your device so the game remembers you. When your device connects to our servers, your IP address is used to deliver the game data; we do not store it in your account.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. The game does not contain ads or third-party analytics. Google Play Games sign-in is provided by Google and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Security

Game data is sent over a standard internet connection that is not encrypted. Because the game does not collect personal information, this data contains only game progress and game IDs.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress is kept while your account is in use. To have your data deleted, email us with your in-game player name and we will delete it. Uninstalling the game removes the player ID from your device.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes (for example, if ads or in-app purchases are added), we will update this policy and change the date above.
